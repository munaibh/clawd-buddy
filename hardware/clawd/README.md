# Clawd case: Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.46B + 500 mAh LiPo

A blocky Clawd: square body with the round screen as its face, a stubby arm
each side and two pairs of legs. 72 x 60 x 21.6 mm with arms and legs. It
**stands on its legs** facing you or **lies on its back** with the screen up.
No stand, no hinge.

The board is mounted upside down so **USB-C comes out of the top of Clawd's
head**; a cable out of the bottom would hit the desk. Flash the
**`waveshare-146b-flipped`** firmware (web flasher: the Clawd case
hardware option; or `npm run upload` from the repo root) so the picture is
the right way up.

Generated by `buddy_clawd.py` (CadQuery) from Waveshare's STEP model and
checked against it (turned 180 degrees): no collisions with the board, the
plungers line up with the side switches.

## Hardware

- 3 x **M2 x 6**: board to plate. No longer than 6 mm. Snug, don't force.
- 4 x **M2 x 6**: back cover into the corner bosses (they self-tap).
- Thin double-sided tape for the battery (tissue or office tape, not foam:
  there's only about 0.3 mm spare above the cell).

## Buttons

The board is round and its two side switches point out along its radius,
35 degrees off the wall. The shell runs a tunnel along each switch's own
axis (see `angled_buttons.png`), out through the left side of Clawd: **PWR
near the bottom, BOOT near the top**.

- **Plungers:** push in from outside, tip first, until the little arms click
  open past the inner end of the tunnel. The pad stays outside, parallel to
  the wall, and stops on it at full press. Slim2 or slim4: the tunnel prints
  tighter than drawn, so the plungers are 0.2 / 0.4 mm thinner than the
  2.4 x 1.6 mm design; use whichever slides in.
- **Key (or a paperclip):** leave the plungers out and push the pin into a
  hole along its angle, towards Clawd's middle, until it clicks, about 17 mm
  in. The tunnel guides it onto the switch.

PWR: hold 3 s for off, press for on. BOOT: a short press turns the screen
off/on.

## Assembly

1. **Check battery polarity** against the BAT socket before plugging in.
2. Board in through the back, glass first into the round face recess, USB-C
   up towards the top of the head.
3. Plate in from the back onto the inner ledge, posts towards the board, and
   screw the board to it with the three M2 x 6.
4. Plungers in from outside (or keep the key handy).
5. Battery lead through the slot in the plate, plug into BAT.
6. Spacer frame in, flat against the plate, round the corner posts.
7. Tape the battery to the back cover inside the frame, at any angle (the
   open area is 52 x 46 mm), with the lead reaching BAT without being pinched.
8. Back cover on, battery side in, four M2 x 6 into the bosses. The frame is
   0.1 mm thicker than the gap, so the last turns pull everything snug. If the
   cover won't close, sand the frame a little; if it still wobbles, add a
   strip of tape on the frame.

## Notes

- Clawd's eyes are drawn on the screen (firmware), not printed.
- Proportions are from memory of Clawd's shape; tweak `U`, `BODY_W`,
  `BODY_H`, `ARM_Y`, `LEGS_X` at the top of `buddy_clawd.py`.
- Clawd is Anthropic's character: fine for your own desk, not for selling.
